## FAQ

**Do I need an appointment to see someone?**
Both walk-ins and scheduled bookings are accepted. Booking through the system guarantees a shorter wait time.

**What should I bring to my visit?**
A valid government-issued photo ID, insurance card (if applicable), and a list of current medications.

**Can the AI assistant provide medical advice or diagnose symptoms?**
No. The assistant is strictly for administrative tasks, scheduling, and visit coordination. Medical symptoms or diagnosis requests are routed to on-site nursing staff or emergency services.

**How do I change or cancel a booked appointment?**
Through the assistant directly, or by calling the front desk.
