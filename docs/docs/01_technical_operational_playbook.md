# Privacy Policy & Data Protection Statement — Sir Tife Health Analysis

**Effective Date:** August 7, 2026

> **⚠️ Prototype disclaimer:** This is a demo privacy policy written for a fictional clinic persona, as part of a portfolio project demonstrating privacy-by-design for healthcare AI. It does not govern any real service.

## 1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines the policies and procedures regarding the collection, use, processing, storage, and disclosure of information when using the administrative assistant, scheduling voice box, and digital triage tools.

## 2. Information Collected & Processed

- **Personal identification information:** full name, contact phone numbers, email addresses, communication preferences
- **Administrative & scheduling data:** desired appointment dates, preferred provider selections, department details, visit purposes
- **Triage & symptom disclosures:** brief symptom descriptions shared exclusively for clinical routing, red-flag safety checks, and emergency escalation
- **Pharmacy requests:** medication inquiries, prescription refill details, interaction logs with pharmacy inventory records

## 3. Lawful Basis & Purpose

Data is processed strictly for legitimate administrative and healthcare coordination purposes: appointment/resource coordination, pharmacy verification, clinical safety and triage routing, and service notifications (schedule adjustments, theater shifts, arrival-time updates).

## 4. Data Security & Confidentiality

- **Strict medical confidentiality:** all patient interactions and health-related disclosures are restricted to authorized administrative and clinical support personnel
- **Encrypted transmission:** secure protocols across all digital and voice interfaces
- **Access control:** strictly role-based — only personnel directly involved in a patient's care coordination can view their details
- **Data retention:** records retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or applicable legal/regulatory obligations

## 5. Sharing & Disclosure

Personal identification and health-related information is never sold, traded, rented, or leased. Disclosure happens only:

- **To internal clinical teams:** physicians, nursing staff, surgical coordinators, and pharmacists directly responsible for the patient's visit or prescription
- **To emergency services:** when a red-flag clinical emergency is matched, relevant triage details are shared immediately with emergency responders or on-site medical staff
- **For legal compliance:** where required by applicable law, court orders, or governmental regulations

## 6. Patient Data Rights

- **Right of access:** request a summary of personal and scheduling data on file
- **Right to correction:** request correction of inaccurate or outdated details
- **Right to withdraw consent:** opt out of non-essential communications, subject to what's required to maintain active bookings

## 7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in administrative workflows, technological capabilities, or legal requirements, with modifications posted alongside an updated effective date.

## 8. Contact

Questions, complaints, or data requests are handled through the main service desk or hospital support portal (demo contact channels only — see [`03_practice_faq.md`](03_practice_faq.md)).
